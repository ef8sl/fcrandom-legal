PURCHASES

Gems are a consumable virtual item with no monetary value. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred between accounts, or redeemed for anything outside the game, and they are consumed when spent.

Premium packs are opened with gems. Each pack shows the odds of every rarity, the chance of a special or HERO card, and any guarantee it carries, before you buy it. Players from premium packs are kept in your Club Vault, where three of them can be fused into one; a fused card's rarity and HERO chance are shown before you confirm.

The STARTER OFFER is a one-time bundle of gems and one LEGENDARY pack, offered once per account.

FC RANDOM PRO is a one-time purchase and does not renew. It removes the sacking so a career runs as long as you want, gives three save slots, adds each month’s exclusive icon to your account permanently from the month it is released, enlarges the Club Vault, unlocks the Player Creator and the Legacy Vault, and gives a head start in the draft and the transfer market. Runs posted with PRO are shown as such on the Hall of Fame.

Card frames, pitch skins and name flair are bought with FAME, which is earned only by playing. FAME cannot be bought and has no monetary value.

All prices are shown by the App Store in your local currency before you confirm. Payment is taken by Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple under their terms, not by us. Restore Purchases in the store returns FC RANDOM PRO after a reinstall. A refunded purchase is removed from the account.

Anyone who bought ENDLESS CAREER before FC RANDOM PRO replaced it keeps it permanently, at no further cost.

Gems are also earned by playing — trophies, seasons survived and manager levels all pay.
