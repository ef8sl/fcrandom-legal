FC RANDOM — Privacy Policy

Poyrazium · Effective 20 October 2026 · f8infoiletisim@gmail.com

THE SHORT VERSION

FC RANDOM stores your manager progress so it survives a reinstall, and posts finished careers to a public leaderboard under the name you choose. It also sends a crash report if the game breaks, so it can be fixed. That is all it collects. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

You can play the entire game offline, without an account, and never appear on the leaderboard.

WHAT IS COLLECTED

Account: an account identifier, and — only if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple — the display name, email address and profile picture that provider returns. Guest accounts collect none of that; they are an anonymous identifier and nothing else.

Manager name: whatever you type. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard, so do not use your real name if you would rather not.

Progress: manager level, experience, career counters, club colours, gem and FAME totals, the looks you own, and purchases.

Finished careers: the result of a run — points, position, goals, division reached, trophies, the seed it was generated from, and a one-line highlight. These are public.

Purchases: a transaction identifier, the product bought, and when. Payment itself is handled by Apple; card details never reach this game or its servers.

Crash reports: when the game crashes, the error, the app version, the device model and your account identifier. No name, email address or IP address is included.

WHAT IS NOT COLLECTED

No advertising identifier. No location. No contacts, photos, microphone or camera. No browsing or app-usage history. No analytics. Nothing is used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, in the sense the App Tracking Transparency framework means.

WHO PROCESSES IT

Supabase provides the database and authentication.

Sentry receives crash reports.

RevenueCat processes in-app purchase receipts and tells the game what was bought.

Apple processes the payment and, if you use Sign in with Apple, the sign-in.

Google processes the sign-in if you choose Google. Each is a processor acting on instructions; none is given data for their own advertising.

HOW LONG IT IS KEPT

Progress and account data are kept until you delete the account. Leaderboard entries are kept as long as the leaderboard exists, or until the account is deleted. Purchase receipts are kept as long as tax and refund obligations require.

YOUR CHOICES

RESET ALL PROGRESS on the Manager screen erases everything stored on the device.

To delete your account and everything attached to it, including leaderboard entries, use DELETE ACCOUNT on the Manager screen. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. You can also contact us at the address below.

Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, export or object to the processing of your data. The same address handles all of it.

CHILDREN

This game is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created one, contact us and it will be removed.
